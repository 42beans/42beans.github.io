FINAN Receipt Intake

A personal receipt and purchase-record tool maintained by Sean Blakely, with local extraction and owner review.

Purpose and current status

The tool is being set up to collect purchase and receipt emails from the owner's Gmail accounts and combine them with phone scans or photos transferred through Google Drive. Extraction runs locally. Recurring ingestion remains disabled until account access, storage, a phone transfer and a real-receipt review have passed their checks.

Google permission

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. This permission can read email messages and settings. It does not allow the app to send, label, move or delete messages. The current authorization helper reads the Gmail profile to verify the connected account.

Data and control

Saved authorizations stay in private local files outside Drive and are excluded from backups. Receipt extraction is local; the app does not send receipt contents to a cloud OCR or AI service. The owner decides which extracted candidates to accept. Read the privacy policy and terms of use for storage, transfer, retention and control details.
