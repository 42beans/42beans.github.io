PM scan, Job Search Alerts and FINAN Receipt Intake

Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal Gmail tools maintained by Sean Blakely. It covers both their Google access and this information website.

Data access and purpose

PM scan uses Gmail's send permission to deliver reports assembled from local routine records. It processes the report's recipient, subject and content and receives Gmail's send result. It does not request inbox-reading permission.

Job Search Alerts uses Gmail's modify permission to support reading job alerts, labeling processed messages and sending personal summaries. The alert reader accesses message identifiers, headers, labels and bodies. It extracts listing details, including titles, employers, locations and links, for the owner's personal job search. The permission covers more mailbox operations than the alert reader performs.

FINAN Receipt Intake requests Gmail's read-only permission for the owner's personal receipt and purchase-record workflow. The permission allows reading email messages and settings, but not sending, labeling, moving or deleting mail. The current setup checks the Gmail account profile. Receipt-email collection is being prepared, and recurring ingestion remains disabled.

Storage and retention

Saved Google authorizations and app credentials are kept in private local files. Extracted job records, reports and logs are kept in the owner's local project storage. They may also be included in the owner's personal backup or synchronization services.

These tools do not impose a fixed automatic retention period. Stored records remain until the owner removes them. Revoking Google access does not erase existing local records, backups or already sent emails.

FINAN's authorizations are kept in private local files outside Drive and are excluded from backups. When ingestion is enabled, receipt evidence, extracted text and purchase records will remain in private local storage. A dedicated Google Drive folder transfers phone scans or photos to the owner's computer. A separate archive backup of purchase records is planned and has not yet been enabled.

Sharing and connected services

Google processes authorization and Gmail API requests. Reports are sent to the owner's selected address, and job-search summaries are sent to the owner.

When the owner uses connected AI assistants for this personal workflow, selected job-alert data, extracted listings or report contents may be processed by those services to read alerts, organize listings, create summaries or send the requested email. Personal backup and synchronization providers may store copies of local records. These uses depend on the owner's chosen services and settings.

The public information website contains only app descriptions, policies and support contacts. Mailbox contents, authorization files and private project records are not published on it.

FINAN Receipt Intake's selected extraction route runs locally. The app does not send receipt contents to a cloud OCR or AI service. Google Drive processes the phone-file transfer described above.

Limited use

Google user data is used to provide the personal reporting, job-alert and receipt-organization functions described here. It is not sold, used for advertising or used by these tools to train general-purpose AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Security and owner control

Google sign-in takes place through Google's authorization flow; these tools do not ask for the owner's Google password. Saved authorization files allow access to the approved Gmail functions and must be protected by the owner's device and backup access controls.

The owner can stop the scripts and remove their access through Google Account permissions. To remove retained data, the owner must also remove the relevant local records and any backup or synchronized copies. Existing emails can be managed in Gmail. Removing local files does not revoke Google access by itself.

This website

The pages contain no analytics scripts, sign-in forms or embedded third-party content. They do not read Gmail or collect visitor submissions. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and privacy statement.
