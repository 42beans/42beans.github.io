PM scan and Job Search Alerts

Terms of use

These tools are maintained by Sean Blakely for his own personal use. They are not a public email or job-application service.

Intended use

PM scan sends personal routine reports. Job Search Alerts reads the owner's job-board alert emails and supports processed-message labels and personal summaries. Use is limited to accounts and data the owner is authorized to access.

Google authorization

Access to Gmail requires the owner's consent through Google. The owner can revoke access at any time. These pages do not grant access, collect passwords or start the scripts.

Reliability and responsibility

Reports and extracted listings can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. The owner should check the original source before making a decision or acting on a job posting. An extracted listing is not an application submission.

The tools are provided as personal utilities without a promise of uninterrupted operation or accuracy. The owner controls when they run, the accounts they access and the records they retain.

Privacy and support

Use of Google data is described in the privacy policy. Support contacts are mixicann@gmail.com for PM scan and seanblakely42@gmail.com for Job Search Alerts.
